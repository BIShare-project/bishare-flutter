### Full — App Store
```
What's new in BIShare:

• Device Dashboard — all your devices in one place. See who's online, check last-seen, rename, favorite, and set auto-accept.
• Universal clipboard for images — copy a picture on one device and paste it on another, plus a clipboard history to grab recent items.
• Invite a device — no one nearby? Show a QR code so friends can install BIShare and connect in seconds.
• Better browser sharing — download whole folders as a zip and resume uploads that got interrupted.
• Rooms open instantly — no more waiting to join or create.
• Smoother, more reliable transfers — progress no longer gets stuck and connections are more dependable.
• Steadier nearby list — devices keep their place, so you won't tap the wrong one by accident.
• Now available in 13 languages.

Questions or feedback? support@billiongroup.net
```
