BIShare sends files between iPhone, Android, Mac, Windows, and Linux — directly over your own network, end-to-end encrypted, at full Wi-Fi speed. No account, no ads, no cloud in the middle.

<b>How it works</b>

• Devices on the same Wi-Fi (or one phone's hotspot — no internet needed) find each other automatically and transfer directly, encrypted with X25519 + AES-256-GCM.
• The other side doesn't need the app: any browser on your network can send and receive.
• Far apart? Create an expiring link with an end-to-end encrypted container.
• Extras: group transfer rooms, universal clipboard between your devices, inbox with gallery and history, QR Beam (send small files as animated QR codes with no network at all).

<b>About this F-Droid build</b>

• Camera QR scanning is disabled in this build: the upstream scanner library bundles Google's proprietary MLKit, which is excluded here. Showing QR codes (QR Beam sending, share codes) still works — only camera scanning is off.
• Anonymous usage telemetry (a transfer count and byte total, no IPs or file names) is opt-in in this build and off by default.
• All local-network features work fully offline. Optional internet features (expiring share links, remote rooms) use BIShare's hosted relay.

Free and open source (MIT). One Dart/Flutter codebase, with all cryptography in a shared Rust core.
